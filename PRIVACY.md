# Privacy

Dispatch collects nothing. No account, no tracking, no ads.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. This app has no internet access. Dispatch only hands what you share to the app you pick.

It has no permission at all, including no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It sees what you share only while its share screen is open, to show a preview and hand it to the app you pick. It does not keep it.

## Only when you choose it

- Copy and Copy image put it on the clipboard. The app you paste into reads it.
- Save writes it into the folder you pick.
- Tapping an app hands it what you shared, and nothing else.
- Links in the About screen (email, sidali.dev) open in your mail app or browser, only when you tap them. Dispatch itself never goes online.

## Stays on this device

- Your pinned apps, in your order, and your hidden apps.
- The "Remember last used" switch. Only while it is on, the last app you used: one entry, replaced each time. No counters, no history.
- Private copies of the last three images you copied with "Copy image", so pasting keeps working. They never leave the app's private storage, except through the clipboard when you paste.
- All of it is excluded from backups and phone to phone transfers.

## Erasing

"Erase everything" in About resets your favorites, hidden apps and settings, and deletes the private copies of copied images. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it too.
