The Android share menu shuffles its apps, repeats them (Feed, Messages, Reels, Stories) and shows people at random. Dispatch is a share menu you arrange yourself.

Pin Dispatch once at the top of the system share menu. From then on, share as usual, tap Dispatch, and your favorites are there, in the order you chose.

<b>Your list</b>
* Add any app that can receive what you share, down to the exact screen: keep Instagram Messages, hide Stories.
* Reorder by touch and hold, or with Move up and Move down.
* Other apps stay one tap away, sorted by name.

<b>Built-in actions</b>
* Copy: links are cleaned of tracking parameters (utm, fbclid, gclid, igshid, si on YouTube and Spotify, mc).
* Copy image: puts the shared image on the clipboard.
* Save: writes what you shared to the folder you pick.

<b>Private by construction</b>
* No permission at all, not even internet access.
* No account, no analytics, no ads. It does not count what you share or where.
* "Remember last used" is off unless you turn it on.

Works on any Android 11+ phone, built and tested for GrapheneOS.
